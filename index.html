04 / 05 · The Toolbench

I build the tools I wish I had.

Self-directed software that makes controls programming faster, more consistent, and easier to verify before it ever reaches a panel.

  • Sandbox PPCLA live PPCL simulator at ppclsimulator.app, with a point database and watch table for testing programs without field equipment.
  • PPCL ForgeReviews a program against its sequence, flags conflicts, lists virtual points, and protects critical safeties.
  • ClarityViewDiscovers BACnet/IP devices, binds live values to graphics, and renders every piece of equipment in 3D, assembled into a live digital twin of the building. The twin below is a simulated taste of it.
  • Desigo Optic GuideAn illustrated setup guide for installing, licensing, and maintaining Desigo Optic 5.3.
